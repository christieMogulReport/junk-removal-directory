A dumpster quote usually looks like one number, but the final bill is that number **plus** whatever happens during the rental: extra weight, extra days, a blocked driveway on pickup day, a mattress in the box, or a city permit. This guide pulls together published prices from national cost guides and large haulers, then walks through every common add-on fee so you can compare quotes fairly.

> **Quick answer.** Angi and HomeAdvisor put the national average at about **$386**, with most people paying **$294 to $480**, and a range of roughly $200 to $800 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/)). Thumbtack's November 2026 data puts the typical range at **$350 to $564** ([Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/p/dumpster-rental-prices)). Dumpsters.com, which reports what its own customers paid, shows higher averages: **$586** for a 10 yard up to **$906** for a 40 yard ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/pricing/roll-off-dumpster-prices)). The most common extra charges are **overage at about $40 to $250 per ton**, **extra days at about $5 to $50 per day**, and **trip fees around $195 to $350** (sources below). These are published ranges, not quotes. Confirm with your hauler and your city.

Not sure what size you need? Start with [Dumpster rental sizes explained](/dumpster-rental-sizes-explained/), which covers dimensions, pickup-truck loads, and weight limits. This page is about the money.

## Flat-rate vs. variable pricing

How a company prices the rental decides which fees you're likely to see.

- **Flat rate (all-inclusive):** one price covers delivery, pickup, the rental window, disposal, and a weight allowance. You pay extra only if you go over the allowance, keep it longer, or toss restricted items. Dumpsters.com and Budget Dumpster both use this model ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/pricing/roll-off-dumpster-prices), [Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)).
- **Variable rate:** delivery, rental, and dump fees are itemized, often after the load is weighed. HomeGuide says the two models tend to cost about the same per week in the end, but variable pricing can come in higher or lower depending on weight and debris ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)).

WM lists the fees that are "common across most dumpster rental companies": a delivery fee, extra days, an overflow fee for debris above the fill line, an overload fee per extra ton, and taxes. It also notes that permit costs are **not** included in its dumpster rental price ([WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/dumpster-rental)).

This is where budgets go wrong. Here are the common add-ons, with published ranges and real fee schedules.

### Overage: the fee most likely to surprise you

Every flat-rate quote includes a weight allowance, often **1 to 5 tons** depending on size ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/blog/what-affects-dumpster-rental-costs)). The box is weighed on landfill scales after pickup. Budget Dumpster says its overage charge usually shows up within 30 days, but can take up to **120 days** depending on when it gets the landfill weight ticket ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)), so don't assume a clean invoice on day one is final.

Heavy debris is the usual cause. The EPA's conversion table puts loose concrete scrap at **1,855 lb per cubic yard**, loose sandy-loam soil at **2,392 lb**, and whole brick at **3,024 lb**, compared with about **419 lb** for loose asphalt shingles ([EPA](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-03/documents/conversions.pdf)). Rain adds weight too. Budget Dumpster suggests tarping the box before a storm, especially if it holds carpet or upholstered furniture ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)). Budget Dumpster also caps all its roll-offs at a **10 ton** maximum for road safety, whatever the allowance ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)). For the full weight table and size-by-weight advice, see [Dumpster rental sizes explained](/dumpster-rental-sizes-explained/).

### Fuel, energy, and environmental charges

Large haulers may add percentage-based charges on top of the base price. How these apply to a one-time residential roll-off depends on the company and your agreement, so ask whether the quote includes them. For reference, the big national haulers publish how theirs work:

- **WM** replaced its fuel surcharge with an **Energy Surcharge** in April 2023. It is a fluctuating percentage of invoice charges tied to U.S. Department of Energy diesel and natural gas prices. WM says its Environmental and Regulatory Cost Recovery charges were folded into base rates for most collection and disposal customers at the same time, though some customers may still see them ([WM Energy Surcharge](https://www.wm.com/us/en/terms/energy-surcharge), [WM charge information](https://www.wm.com/us/en/terms/charge-information)).
- **Republic Services** lists an **Environmental Recovery Fee** with a current standard rate of **18%** for hauling services, a fluctuating **Fuel Recovery Fee** tied to diesel prices, and a **$5.95 per invoice** administrative fee. It says these are company fees, not government taxes, and may vary by location or contract ([Republic Services fee disclosures](https://www.republicservices.com/customer-support/fee-disclosures)).
- **Smaller haulers** may list fuel surcharges in their terms. Dumpee's terms, for example, include fuel surcharges, distance or trip fees, and taxes ([Dumpee](https://dumpeedumpsters.com/company-policies/)).

Ask for the **out-the-door total**, including taxes, fuel, and any environmental or admin fees, in writing.

## Permits: what cities actually charge

If the box sits entirely on your own driveway, many cities don't require a permit. On a street, sidewalk, or alley, most do. Published national ranges: **$10 to $100** (Angi, HomeAdvisor), **$10 to $75** (HomeGuide), and **$20 to $150**, possibly more in major cities (Budget Dumpster, Dumpsters.com) ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/), [HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices), [Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php), [Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/blog/what-affects-dumpster-rental-costs)).

Real city examples show how much the rules vary:

| City | Who applies | Published fee |
|---|---|---|
| [Saint Paul, MN](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/public-works/right-way/dumpster-permits) | Only a licensed solid waste hauler can apply; residents can't. No permit needed if the box is fully on private property | $10 per day; $18 per day downtown (non-metered) |
| [Savannah, GA](https://www.savannahga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1946/Dumpster-Permit-?bidId=) | Applicant at Traffic Engineering; four working days to process | $20 application fee plus $4 per parking space per day (excluding Sundays and holidays); $10 penalty if issued after enforcement |
| [San Diego, CA](https://www.sandiego.gov/development-services/forms-publications/information-bulletin/718) | Traffic control permit with a plan; at least three working days' notice; post no-parking signs 72 hours ahead | $176.57 per month dumpster fee plus an $87.68 processing fee; $20 per metered space per day |

So a week on the street could be about $70 in Saint Paul, but about $264 in San Diego for a one-month dumpster permit plus the processing fee, before any meter charges. That is our arithmetic from the published fees, not a quote. Your hauler may pull the permit for you and add it to the bill, or you may have to apply yourself. HomeGuide says approval can take up to three days ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)), and HOAs can have their own rules. Call your city's public works or right-of-way office to confirm.

## How to get an accurate quote (and avoid surprises)

1. **Describe the debris honestly.** Household junk, remodel debris, yard waste, roofing, or concrete? Dumpsters.com says different debris goes to different facilities with different disposal fees ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/pricing/roll-off-dumpster-prices)).
2. **Ask how many tons are included and the overage rate per ton.** HomeGuide suggests asking about overage and whether pricing is fixed or variable ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)).
3. **Ask how many days are included and the daily extension rate.**
4. **List any mattresses, tires, appliances, or electronics** and ask for per-item fees in your area.
5. **Ask about the trip fee and what counts as a blocked or overfilled box.** Budget Dumpster says its drivers usually need about 60 feet of straight-line space, 10.5 feet of width, and 25 to 30 feet of vertical clearance ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)).
6. **Ask who handles the permit** if the box can't go on your property.
7. **Ask for the full total in writing**, including delivery, pickup, taxes, dump fees, mileage, fuel, and admin fees. HomeGuide recommends confirming each of these is included ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)).
8. **Get at least three quotes.** Angi and HomeAdvisor both recommend comparing local companies ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/)).
9. **Watch for scams.** A rock-bottom price, payment by gift card or payment app only, or a company you can't verify are warning signs. See [Junk removal and dumpster rental scams: red flags](/junk-removal-and-dumpster-rental-scams-red-flags/).

## Ways to save

- **Pick the right size.** Too big wastes money, too small risks overage or a second haul. A size up often costs little more ([Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/p/dumpster-rental-prices), [Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm)).
- **Keep heavy debris separate** and ask about a heavy-material box for concrete, brick, or dirt.
- **Donate or sell first** to shrink the load ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/)).
- **Schedule tightly** and request pickup as soon as you're done ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/), [Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/p/dumpster-rental-prices)).
- **Rent off-season.** Angi says fewer dumpsters are available in spring, so an off-season rental may come with a discount ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm)).
- **Ask about discounts.** Angi mentions military, senior, and first responder discounts at some companies ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm)). HomeGuide says some local companies post $50 to $100 off a first rental ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)).
- **Keep the box on your property** to avoid a street permit where possible, and keep the delivery spot clear to avoid a trip fee.
- **Tarp the box** before rain or snow ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)).

Dumpsters.com's range is $329 to $1,307 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/), [HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices), [Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/pricing/roll-off-dumpster-prices)).

**Can I rent a dumpster for just one day?** Some companies offer it. Angi lists daily rates of $40 to $100, but HomeGuide says most companies require at least a week, and Dumpsters.com says a shorter period usually doesn't lower the price ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices), [Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/blog/what-affects-dumpster-rental-costs)).

**Are delivery and pickup included?** With flat-rate companies, yes. Dumpsters.com and Budget Dumpster bundle delivery, pickup, the rental period, and disposal up to a weight limit. WM lists a delivery fee among common fees, so ask ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/pricing/roll-off-dumpster-prices), [Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php), [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/dumpster-rental)).

**Is the landfill (tipping) fee included?** Usually, up to the weight limit, on flat-rate rentals. Variable-rate companies may bill it separately after weighing ([Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/blog/what-affects-dumpster-rental-costs), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/rent-a-dumpster/)). Angi and HomeAdvisor put landfill fees at about $20 to $50 per ton.

**What happens if I go over the weight limit?** You pay a per-ton overage fee, typically $40 to $250 per ton depending on the source and market. The box may be refused if it is dangerously heavy ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-dumpster-rental.htm), [Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)).

**Do I need a deposit?** Some companies ask for one. HomeGuide says deposits of $200 to $400 are applied to the final payment ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/dumpster-rental-prices)).

**Why did my bill show up weeks later?** Overage and item fees depend on the landfill's weight ticket. Budget Dumpster says those can take up to 120 days to post ([Budget Dumpster](https://www.budgetdumpster.com/resources/dumpster-fees.php)).
