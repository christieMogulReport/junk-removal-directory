## What this site is

Junk & Dumpster Guide is a small independent publisher of how-to guides about junk removal and dumpster rental. It is run by Black Swan Sibyl (Christie Justice). The goal is to help you pick the right option, avoid surprise fees, and ask the right questions before you book.

## How we research

- Prices, sizes, and weight limits come from named public sources, which are linked at the bottom of each guide.
- We show ranges, not single numbers, and say when sources disagree. Local prices vary a lot, so treat every range as a starting point and get a written quote.
- We do not make up numbers. If we could not find a reliable source, the guide says so.
- Each page shows a "last updated" date. If something looks out of date, tell us.

## Not professional advice

Guides are general information. They are not legal, environmental, or safety advice. Disposal rules (hazardous waste, electronics, appliances with refrigerant, permits for street placement) differ by location. Check with your city or county and with your hauler.
