Junk removal companies don't post one national price list. Most full-service haulers price by **how much space your stuff takes up in their truck**, quote on site, and add fees for certain items. Others price **item by item online**. This guide pulls together the published numbers: national averages, truckload tiers, single-item prices, minimum charges, and common surcharges. It also covers what pushes a quote up or down and how to get a price you can count on.

> **Quick answer.** Angi's 2026 data puts the national average at **$241**, with most people paying **$133 to $372** ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). A **single bulky item** usually has a minimum charge of about **$60 to $150**, and a **full truckload** runs about **$600 to $800** ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)). Expect add-on fees for some items, such as tires, TVs, mattresses, and appliances with refrigerant, and for heavy debris like concrete or dirt. These are published ranges, not quotes. Your local price can be higher or lower, so confirm with your hauler.

Trying to decide between a junk removal crew and a roll-off dumpster? See [Junk removal vs. dumpster rental: which is cheaper?](/junk-removal-vs-dumpster-rental-which-is-cheaper/). This page goes deeper on junk removal pricing itself.

## How junk removal companies price a job

There are three common models. Knowing which one a company uses tells you what kind of quote to expect.

| Pricing model | How it works | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Volume (truckload fraction)** | Price is based on how much of the truck your items fill. Quoted on site after the crew sees the pile | 1-800-GOT-JUNK charges in ⅛-truck increments with a free on-site estimate ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)). College HUNKS prices by the volume your items take up in its truck ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)) |
| **Single item** | One item gets its own flat price based on its size | 1-800-GOT-JUNK prices a single item by size, so "a dresser would cost more than a microwave" ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)) |
| **Item-based online pricing** | You pick items online and get a guaranteed price, plus a per-pickup fee | LoadUp adds one area service fee per pickup, not per item ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)). 1-800-GOT-JUNK's Flex service gives a guaranteed online price for items left outside, "starting at just $39" ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)) |

Most volume-priced companies say their quote is all-inclusive. 1-800-GOT-JUNK lists loading, clean-up, and disposal and environmental fees as included ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)). College HUNKS says its price covers labor, gas, and drop-off at a charity or disposal site ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/why-us/faq/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost/)). Even so, item surcharges and heavy-material rules can still apply (see below).

## Minimum fees

Almost every company has a floor price, even for a small pickup:

- Angi says you may have to pay for a **minimum of about 2 cubic yards**, roughly ⅛ to ¼ of a truck, even if your load is smaller ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- For furniture-only jobs, Angi says most pros charge a **minimum flat fee of $75 to $150**, then price each extra piece by volume and weight ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- For electronics, Angi and HomeAdvisor say most haulers charge a **flat trip fee of $75 to $100** no matter how little you have, so it pays to wait until you have several pieces ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)).
- HomeGuide's 2024 table lists a minimum load of up to 60 cubic feet at **$70 to $100** ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/junk-removal-prices)).

Local examples: Fire Dawgs Junk Removal (Indiana, Ohio, and Houston) starts at **$99 for curbside pickup** and has a **$149 minimum** for full-service removal ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)). Same Day Haulers lists a **$125 minimum pickup fee**, which covers up to 5 contractor bags or one standard couch ([Same Day Haulers](https://www.samedayhaulers.com/pricing)). Ready Now charges **$99 for a single item** from a first-floor or curbside spot ([Ready Now](https://readynowjunkremoval.com/pricing)).

## What drives the price

**1. Volume.** This is the biggest factor. More truck space means a higher price, and big jobs can need more than one truck ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm), [1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)).

**2. Labor and access.** Angi puts labor at **$100 to $300** of a typical bill and says stairs and tight hallways push it toward the high end ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). LoadUp adds charges for flights of stairs, heavy or bulky items, and disassembly ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)). Ready Now says stairs, long carries, or difficult access may add a fee ([Ready Now](https://readynowjunkremoval.com/pricing)). Fire Dawgs lists flat fees for hard-to-move items, such as **$250 to $500** for a treadmill inside the house and **$300 to $550** for a piano ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)).

**3. Heavy materials.** Concrete, dirt, and shingles are dense, so a small pile can be very heavy. 1-800-GOT-JUNK says these loads have **dedicated weight limits** ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)), and College HUNKS prices construction and demolition debris by **volume and weight** ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)). Angi puts construction debris at **$100 to $800** per truckload ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). Expect an in-person estimate for this kind of job.

**4. Item surcharges.** Some items cost the hauler extra to recycle or dispose of, and that fee is passed on to you. Common ones:

| Item | Published surcharge examples |
|---|---|
| Tires | HomeAdvisor: about $5–$10 per tire without rims and $10–$15 with rims at a recycler or tire shop. A junk removal company will charge a minimum of $60, which includes state tire fees of $0.25–$2 per tire ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)). Fire Dawgs: $15–$30 per car tire ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)). Ready Now: $9 per car or light truck tire ([Ready Now](https://readynowjunkremoval.com/pricing)) |
| TVs | Same Day Haulers: $50 per TV ([Same Day Haulers](https://www.samedayhaulers.com/pricing)). Fire Dawgs: $20–$70 for a projection TV ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)) |
| Mattresses and box springs | 1-800-GOT-JUNK says items like tires and mattresses may have eco-fees ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)). Same Day Haulers: $95 each ([Same Day Haulers](https://www.samedayhaulers.com/pricing)) |
| Appliances with refrigerant (fridges, freezers, AC units) | HomeAdvisor: refrigerant (freon) removal and disposal $50–$150 ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)). Ready Now: $25 per fridge, freezer, or AC unit, matching its local landfill's fee ([Ready Now](https://readynowjunkremoval.com/pricing)) |
| Paint and chemicals | College HUNKS lists paint among items with extra landfill disposal fees ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)). Same Day Haulers estimates $15–$35 per gallon ([Same Day Haulers](https://www.samedayhaulers.com/pricing)). Many haulers won't take hazardous materials at all |
| Hot tubs | College HUNKS says hot tubs may cost more because of disassembly time ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)). Angi: $200–$600 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)) |

Some items are refused outright. See [What junk removal companies won't take](/what-junk-removal-companies-wont-take/) before you book.

**5. Disposal fees.** Local dump fees are built into the price. Angi says landfill fees typically run **$50 to $100 per ton**, and **$250 to $500 per ton** for hazardous waste. It suggests asking whether disposal fees are included in the quote or billed separately ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).

**6. Location.** Angi says you could pay **10% to 20% more or less** than the national average depending on where you live, mostly because of labor costs ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). College HUNKS says its rates are set by location to reflect local wages and disposal costs ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)).

**7. Timing.** Angi says costs can be a little higher in spring and early summer, when many renovation projects happen, and that rush jobs may cost a small premium ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). Some companies also charge for late changes. Fire Dawgs, for example, lists a $100 same-day cancellation fee ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)).

## How to get an accurate quote

1. **Make a list and take photos.** Junk King offers estimates by text photo, but says a photo estimate is not a firm price. By phone, it may give a firm price for four items or fewer ([Junk King](https://www.junk-king.com/pricing)).
2. **Ask for an on-site estimate for anything bigger.** 1-800-GOT-JUNK and College HUNKS both give free, no-obligation on-site estimates. You can say no ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing), [College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)).
3. **Mention stairs, long carries, heavy items, and anything on the surcharge list up front.** These are the usual reasons a quote changes.
4. **Ask what's included.** HomeGuide suggests confirming the quote covers pickup, loading, taxes, dump fees, and mileage, and asking about hazardous waste and e-waste surcharges ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/junk-removal-prices)).
5. **Get the final price before work starts.** Reputable companies confirm the price before loading. Be wary of a crew that won't put a number on it, raises the price after the truck is full, or wants cash only. See [Junk removal and dumpster rental scams: red flags](/junk-removal-and-dumpster-rental-scams-red-flags/).
6. **Compare at least three quotes.** Angi recommends talking with at least three local companies ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). Junk King advertises that it will beat any written estimate ([Junk King](https://www.junk-king.com/pricing)), so a written quote from a competitor can be worth having.

## Ways to save

- **Put items outside.** LoadUp takes $5 off for curbside pickup ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)). 1-800-GOT-JUNK says outdoor pickup gets its lowest price ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing)). Fire Dawgs' curbside price starts lower than its full-service minimum ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)). Only move what you can lift safely.
- **Gather everything in one spot.** Angi says this cuts lifting and sorting time ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- **Book one bigger pickup instead of several small ones.** Minimums and per-pickup fees mean the first item costs the most. LoadUp's volume discounts go up to 10% as you add items ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)).
- **Check your city's bulk pickup first.** Angi notes many cities include bulk pickup in regular trash service ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- **Donate what's usable.** Angi and HomeAdvisor point to charities like Habitat for Humanity, and HomeAdvisor says some will pick up gently used furniture or appliances for free ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)).
- **Use free hazardous waste drop-offs.** Angi says local household hazardous waste programs often charge no disposal fee if you bring the items yourself ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- **Avoid peak season and rush jobs** when you can ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)).
- **Look for coupons.** For example, Junk King posted a $30-off coupon through Dec. 31, 2026, at participating locations, not valid below the minimum ([Junk King](https://www.junk-king.com/pricing)).
- **Consider a dumpster for big or heavy jobs.** If you can do the loading, a roll-off can cost less for large cleanouts. Compare in our [junk removal vs. dumpster guide](/junk-removal-vs-dumpster-rental-which-is-cheaper/), check prices and hidden fees in [how much does a dumpster rental cost?](/how-much-does-a-dumpster-rental-cost/), and pick a size with [dumpster rental sizes explained](/dumpster-rental-sizes-explained/).

## FAQ

**What's the minimum charge for junk removal?** Angi puts the minimum for a single bulky item at $60 to $150, and says you may be charged for at least about 2 cubic yards even for a small load ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). Local minimums vary. For example, $125 at Same Day Haulers and $149 for full service at Fire Dawgs ([Same Day Haulers](https://www.samedayhaulers.com/pricing), [Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)).

**How much does a full truckload of junk removal cost?** Angi and HomeAdvisor put a full truckload (13 to 17 cubic yards) at $600 to $800 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm), [HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)). Truck sizes differ by company, so ask how big the truck is.

**How much does it cost to get rid of a couch?** Angi's 2026 average is $100 to $200 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost.htm)). Online item pricing can be lower in some areas. LoadUp's sample for one Atlanta ZIP code shows $30 for a couch plus a $59 service fee per pickup ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)).

**Why are there extra fees for tires, TVs, and mattresses?** Disposal and recycling facilities charge haulers for these items, and many haulers pass the fee on. College HUNKS mentions extra landfill fees for items like tires and paint ([College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/pricing/junk-removal-pricing/)), and Fire Dawgs says its asterisked fees are what recycling centers charge it ([Fire Dawgs](https://firedawgsjunkremoval.com/surcharge-list/)).

**Is the price on the phone or online the final price?** It depends on the company. LoadUp says its online price is guaranteed as long as the items match what's picked up ([LoadUp](https://goloadup.com/pricing/)). Junk King says phone and online estimates are confirmed on site with a firm, no-obligation price ([Junk King](https://www.junk-king.com/pricing)). Volume-priced companies usually set the final price when they see the items.

**Is disposal included in the price?** At the big franchises, yes. 1-800-GOT-JUNK includes disposal and environmental fees, and College HUNKS includes drop-off at a charity or disposal site ([1-800-GOT-JUNK](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/pricing), [College HUNKS](https://www.collegehunkshaulingjunk.com/why-us/faq/how-much-does-junk-removal-cost/)). Item surcharges can still apply, so ask.

**Should I tip the crew?** HomeAdvisor says tipping isn't required, but many people give $20 to $50, or 15% to 20%, for hard jobs like stairs or extra-bulky items ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/remove-waste/)).
