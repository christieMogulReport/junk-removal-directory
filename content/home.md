Cleanouts, moves, and renovations all end the same way: a pile of stuff that has to go somewhere. The two main options are **renting a roll-off dumpster** and filling it yourself, or **hiring a junk removal crew** that loads a truck for you. Which one is cheaper depends on how much you have, how heavy it is, and how much of the work you want to do.

These guides explain the choices in plain English. Prices and weight limits come from named public sources and are labeled as ranges, because they change by city and by hauler. Always get a written quote before you book.

*General information only. Rules about what you may dispose of, permits, and fees differ by city, county, and company, so confirm with your local hauler and your city.*
